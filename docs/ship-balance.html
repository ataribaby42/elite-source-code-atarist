04

Impact inspector

Follow individual hits with the current fractional damage calculation. Recovery, misses and ECM are excluded from this controlled comparison.

Each burst draws a uniform duration from 6 through 18 steps (0.36–1.08 seconds at full PAL speed). The first damage check is immediate. Visual-only frames do not cause damage; misses do not interrupt the beam. Timers also advance off-screen. Lost aim, changed/lost targets and the existing firing guards stop a burst. Every incoming damaging laser hit retriggers its impact sound, subject to Effects/music settings.

Accepted-hit decision

  1. Existing targeting, station-space, cloak and control-lock checks apply. Range must be within 12,288 units.
  2. After the cooldown expires, a random byte must be at or below the shooter’s mood to start a Pulse shot or a new burst. A running burst does not repeat this trigger roll.
  3. Direction alignment below 14563/16384 produces no shot. Up to 15928/16384 it produces a visible miss; at or above that threshold a scheduled damage check becomes a candidate hit. Aim is checked on every visible burst step.
  4. A uniform 0–199 roll then applies the distance miss chance below. Interpolation uses half-percentage-point steps.

Collision damage matrix

Full-charge collisions preserve their previous results. Laser and missile resistances do not replace collision strength or the size-class rules.

Base = 8 + floor(legacy collision energy / 32) → size adjustment → round(base × 7 / player strength)

Two or more classes larger: fatal. One class larger: add 4. Equal class: unchanged. One class smaller: integer half. Two or more smaller: integer quarter. Constrictor contact is always fatal.

Collision classes and legacy collision strength are unchanged by the weapon rebalance. Surviving colliders can flash on the facing active shield (orange for Thargoids and Thargons/Tharglets, light blue for other ships); a ship destroyed by the collision does not flash.

Damage calculation

Weapon damage in 1/65536-point units is ceil(base power × 154 × 65536 / resistance). Lasers and missiles select separate resistances. Damage first drains the selected shield, then energy. Worm has no shields, so damage goes directly to energy. The displayed integer is the ceiling of actual remaining energy; the fractional debt is retained. Zero energy is lethal.

Ordinary laser resistance is 14 × the Unbound Pulse count. Cougar and Constrictor use 1122, from the Military ratio 51/7. Missile resistance is 77 × the requested direct-impact count. Collision calculations retain their original separate rounding.

Verification · 3 October 2026

The burst and impact-audio regression run passed 4,041 native scenarios per platform plus 34 ordinary tests; one existing artwork identity check was skipped. All 20 distribution builds passed. Coverage includes burst timing and visible pixels, actual sound-driver restarts, loadout selection, missions and rewards, Thargoids/Tharglets, shared combat, spawn paths, missile and shield effects, flight, cockpit indicators and all game meshes.

Native checks ran on standard 1 MB Atari ST and framed PAL Amiga builds using verified private hidden desktops. Amiga audio, identification-indicator, shield-effect and missile-impact tests used cycle-exact 68000 timing; other native suites used accelerated execution. Other display variants received build/layout verification. All 110 comparison counts still match native results; Worm has no shields and is destroyed by the sixth Pulse hit without recharge.
